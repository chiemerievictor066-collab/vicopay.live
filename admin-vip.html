When a VIP opens a live, the videos play in this order, then the notice slides in from the right, stays 3 seconds and leaves to the left. If a person qualifies for several entrances, the one for the highest tier is used. Videos are the files named gift-<name>.mp4 in your site folder.

VIP gifts

Gifts are sold in coins and paid to the host as Golden Stars, exactly like normal gifts. 1 coin = ₦10, so ₦500 = 50 coins. A gift only appears once it has a price and is switched on, and only while the VIP system is ON.
