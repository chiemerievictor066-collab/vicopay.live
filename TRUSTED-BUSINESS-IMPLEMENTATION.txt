VICO PAY LIVE — TRUSTED BUSINESS / BUSINESS CENTER UPDATE

WHAT CHANGED
- Added a third Business Center tab: Big Business, alongside Company Products and National Products.
- Added Follow, Like, Share and View counters to product cards.
- Follow uses the existing Vico account follow system.
- Likes use the existing product_likes table and are unique per signed-in account per listing.
- Views are recorded when a signed-in user opens a product detail page. A viewer is counted once per listing (unique account), so refreshing does not inflate the number.
- Added customer reviews to product detail pages. Only customers with a completed order for that exact product can submit a review. Reviews are pending until admin moderation.
- Added Vico Trusted Business progress page: vico-trusted-business.html.
- Added admin trust/review moderation page: admin-trusted-business.html, linked from admin.html.

TRUST ELIGIBILITY RULES IMPLEMENTED
- At least 1,000 followers for the seller account.
- At least 1,000,000 likes aggregated across the seller's active marketplace products.
- At least 20 approved customer reviews.
- Average approved review rating at least 4.0 / 5.
- After these milestones, the account is queued and a notification links to vico-trusted-business.html.
- The Vico Trusted Business badge is NOT automatically awarded by counts alone. Admin must complete business, delivery, reputation and fraud checks and manually approve the account. This avoids falsely certifying a company just because it has high engagement.

ADMIN
- Open /admin-trusted-business.html and enter the existing ADMIN_KEY.
- Review milestone applications and approve / reject / place under review.
- Moderate pending customer reviews in the same page.

CUSTOMER
- Open /vico-trusted-business.html to see live milestone progress and trust status.
- Product detail pages now show reviews and a review submission area.

DATABASE
The vico-engage.js module creates these PostgreSQL tables automatically:
- product_views
- product_reviews
- trusted_business_status
Existing product_likes, follows and notifications are reused.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- Likes and views count signed-in Vico accounts. One account counts once per listing; this reduces refresh and repeat-click inflation, but does not by itself prove that an account is a genuine person.
- Reviews require a completed Vico order and admin moderation. The admin must still assess suspicious patterns and complaints.
- The current view metric counts unique signed-in product detail viewers, not simultaneous live watchers.
- No artificial starting counts or fake engagement are inserted.
- This ZIP was syntax-checked and existing market/commerce/company-verification tests passed. It has not been connected to the user's live Render/PostgreSQL deployment in this environment.
