How monthly payouts work

  • You need 3,000 Golden Stars earned in the month.
  • You must go live 3 hours every day of the month.
  • $9 for every full 3,000 stars: 3,000–5,999 stars = $9, 6,000–8,999 = $18, 9,000 = $27 and so on.
  • Payouts are checked after the month ends and sent to your bank account once approved.

Your bank account (Nigeria)

We verify the account with Paystack and show you the account holder's name. If you change your account later, payouts for you are paused for 72 hours as a security check.
