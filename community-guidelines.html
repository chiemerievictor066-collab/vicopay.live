Community Guidelines

Last updated:

VicoPay Live is for everyone. These rules keep the community safe, friendly and fun for creators and viewers alike.

1. Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, hate speech or threats. Treat hosts, viewers and moderators the way you want to be treated.

2. Keep it safe

No nudity, sexual content, graphic violence, or content that promotes self-harm, dangerous acts or illegal activity. Users must be 18 or older to go live or send gifts.

3. Protect children

Content that exploits or endangers minors is banned and reported to the authorities.

4. Be honest

No scams, fake giveaways, impersonation, spam, or misleading investment promises. Forex trading involves risk – never promise guaranteed profits.

5. Respect privacy and ownership

Do not share other people's personal information, and only stream music, video or images you have the right to use.

6. Gifts and payments

Do not ask viewers for money outside VicoPay, use stolen cards, or try to cheat the gift, coin or ranking systems. Fraud leads to frozen wallets and permanent bans.

7. What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may give a warning, end a live, remove content, freeze a wallet or ban the account. You can report anything from the live room, or contact us at the Help Center.
