About VicoPay Live

Live • Chat • Connect • Earn

VicoPay Live is a live-streaming community where creators go live, meet people from around the world, receive virtual gifts and earn from the support of their fans. It also brings together a global community of forex traders who learn and grow together.

🎥 Go LiveStart a live room in seconds, chat with your viewers and build your audience.
🎁 Virtual GiftsViewers send gifts to show support. Hosts earn stars that can become real payouts.
⚔️ PK BattlesCompete head-to-head with other hosts in friendly live battles.
📈 Forex CommunityLearn, share and grow with traders from around the world.

Our mission

To give every creator a safe, fair and rewarding place to share their talent, and to give every viewer a friendly place to enjoy it.

What we stand for

Safety first – we keep the community respectful (see our Community Guidelines).
Fair earnings – coins, gifts and payouts are recorded on a transparent ledger.
Real support – our team replies to every ticket from the Help Center.
