Send a partnership request

Send an inquiry to the company using its registered VicoPay account email. The business decides whether to accept or reject.

How your payment works

1. The business accepts your request and records the amount you both agree on.

2. The agreed amount appears as Pending in your partnership wallet while you create the video and await review.

3. Submit your video for business review. After approval and verified company funding, the amount is recorded as available partnership earnings.

The amount and currency are agreed by the creator and business. The company must pay through verified Paystack checkout. After video approval, NGN payments are transferred to the creator’s verified bank account. Add your bank details in Payout Setup. USD earnings remain in the partnership wallet until a USD bank payout route is configured.

Business requests & video reviews

Only the business account addressed by a request can accept, reject, make an offer or review the submitted video.
