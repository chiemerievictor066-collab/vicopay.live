💳 Check a payment

Paid for coins or the Forex Academy but nothing arrived? Paste the payment reference from your Paystack receipt or email and we will check it with Paystack and apply it if it was successful. It is safe to try more than once: a payment is only ever applied one time.

  • The reference is in the receipt email from Paystack, and in the address bar after you pay (the part after reference=).
  • Please do not pay a second time before checking here.
