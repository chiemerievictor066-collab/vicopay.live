What we collect

Account: your email, optional username and a password that is stored only as a salted hash (we cannot read it).
Activity: coin purchases and balance, gifts sent and received, Golden Stars, live-session start/end times, follows and withdrawal requests.
Payout details: the account name, bank name and account number you enter to request a withdrawal.
Live video and audio: streamed in real time through LiveKit while you are live or watching.

How we use it

To run your account, deliver live streams, process payments and payouts, prevent fraud and abuse, and answer support requests. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Paystack processes payments. LiveKit carries live video and audio. Each handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Public information

Your live room title, category and viewer count are visible to other users. Leaderboards show a shortened version of your identifier.

Keeping data safe

Passwords are hashed, sessions are signed, and payment cards are never stored on our servers. No online service can promise perfect security.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account data by contacting us, subject to records we must keep for payments and fraud prevention.
